# Code of Conduct

The SkyCloudCollective is a place to make music and build tools together. It only works
if it's safe and welcoming for everyone.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body, disability, neurodivergence, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Be kind, patient, and assume good faith.
- Welcome newcomers; remember everyone was new once.
- Be clear and calm; prefer a helpful note to a sharp one.
- Be mindful of accessibility — in your words and in what you build. Many of us are
  neurodivergent; legibility, patience, and low-pressure communication matter.
- Credit others' work. Give feedback on the work, not the person.

Things that are not OK:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption, and uploading content you don't have the rights to.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the app, the repo, chat, and any space where you
represent the project — and to private interactions when they affect a community member.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers (admin handles in the app; or a private repo
contact). Reports are handled discreetly and in good faith. Maintainers may warn,
remove content, or remove someone from project spaces, proportionate to the situation,
and will explain decisions where they can.
